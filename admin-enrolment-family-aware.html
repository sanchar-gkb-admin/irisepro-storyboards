  • Adaptive fields: the form reads Challenge::onboardingFamily() — educators lose class/board/guardian; institution becomes required for institutional (or any external-funded) enrolment.
  • All 3 doors, one contract: single-enrol, bulk-CSV, and batch-create share this resolver + family rules (the CSV Stream column resolves the same way; institution required when institutional).
  • Held invariant — money: public checkout keeps sellable() (is_listed=true); institutional stays off the storefront.
  • Held invariant — demo: demos remain a first-class, labelled enrol target. Nothing regresses.
