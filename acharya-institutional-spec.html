AStreams model & the B2B/B2C refactor

A.1 — The model (verified)

A "stream" = a program_key. Source of truth: SellableChallengeSeeder.php. PROGRAMS = 7 public/B2C (× 3 tiers = "exactly 21 offers"); INSTITUTIONAL_PROGRAMS = admin-only/B2B (the Acharya streams). Today the split is derived into two columns in ChallengeController::applyAudienceAndListing(): audience='acharya'|'student' + is_listed.

A.2 / A.3 — Refactor to a single is_institutional flag

The /institutional list will grow past teachers (Schools, Colleges, CSR, Government, Corporate). Tagging a corporate stream audience='acharya' is wrong. Replace audience + is_listed with one is_institutional boolean, derived from INSTITUTIONAL_PROGRAMS membership. No data migration (re-derives from program_key; no live acharya challenge in prod). The teacher-specific content (study guide, "Acharya" labels) keys on the stream family (program_key LIKE acharya_%), not on is_institutional — so a future Corporate stream never inherits the teacher manual.
