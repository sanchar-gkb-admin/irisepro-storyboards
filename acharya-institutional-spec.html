AStreams model & the B2B/B2C refactor

A.1 — The model today (verified)

A "stream" = a program_key. Single source of truth: database/seeders/SellableChallengeSeeder.php.

  • PROGRAMS — 7 public / B2C (marks_booster, board_booster, neet, jee, ias, kas, career_readiness). Self-enrolled, on storefront, × 3 tiers = "exactly 21 offers".
  • INSTITUTIONAL_PROGRAMS — admin-only / B2B (the 2 Acharya streams). No self-enrolment, off storefront, tier chosen at admin-create.

Today the split is two derived columns in ChallengeController::applyAudienceAndListing():

$isInstitutional = array_key_exists($challenge->program_key, INSTITUTIONAL_PROGRAMS);
$challenge->audience  = $isInstitutional ? 'acharya' : 'student';   // stringly-typed, teacher-specific
$challenge->is_listed = ! $isInstitutional;                          // storefront visibility

A.2 — Why refactor (your call)

The /institutional page shows the B2B list will grow beyond teachers — Schools, Colleges, CSR, Government, Corporate, Leadership. Tagging a corporate or government stream audience='acharya' is plainly wrong: "acharya" means teacher. The flag we actually branch on is the coarse B2B-vs-B2C distinction, so name it that.

A.3 — The change

FromTo
audience string ('student'|'acharya')
is_listed boolean		 is_institutional boolean — the one coarse B2B gate, DERIVED from INSTITUTIONAL_PROGRAMS membership
  • No data migration. The flag re-derives from program_key at create/edit, exactly as audience does now, and there is no live acharya challenge in prod. New migration adds is_institutional; a follow-up drops audience+is_listed once readers move.
  • Fold is_listed into is_institutional (institutional ⇒ unlisted) — no "hide a B2C stream from store while keeping it B2C" case exists today.

D.3 — The three Acharya-specific engine changes

① One saffron benchmark schema change

Student streams carry three layers — LAYERS = (developmental, champion, competitive) — threaded through pillar_comparison, gap_analysis, intelligraph_comparison, priority_improvements. Acharya shows one: the All-India ASLI Champion Benchmark, saffron.

Design: per-stream benchmark_mode: "triple" (default, today) | "single" (champion-only; label "All-India ASLI Champion Benchmark"; saffron).

Touches: benchmarks.py · benchmark_compare.py (emit 1 layer when single) · render template (1 saffron series + legend) · validate.py.

② No Career Pathways (§7) flag

Confirmed from the cards: the educator card has no Career Pathways — its "Section 7" is the Final Conclusion & Success Readiness Summary (= the engine's §8). But build_21task_scorecard always emits section7_career_pathways.

Design: per-stream has_pathways (default true). Acharya = false → §7 skipped + §8's "top pathway" line suppressed. Render already tolerates absent sections.

Touches: benchmarks.py · assemble.py build_21task · render template.

③ Stream-specific Top-15 educator abilities + §1-F notes data ready

Each stream already declares its own top15 and §1-F rationale. Acharya gets an educator Top-15 (Part E). Verified: all 19 distinct abilities across both cards are canonical AACE64 names, and every pillar assignment in the cards matches the engine's canon.py pillar map exactly → data-only, no canon extension, no rubric change (as you said: "the scoring rubric stays the same").

D.4 — What does NOT change (verified)

  • Task→ability mapping and the AACE64 rubric. ✔
  • The §1–§8 assemblers — reused; Acharya flips two per-stream flags.

FTask authoring ↔ engine coupling

The coupling to keep honest: a task's headline_abilities (≤5 keys on tasks) and the engine's §1-F Top-15 for that stream must draw from the same ability set. If Sutirtha authors an Acharya task around an ability outside the stream's Top-15/64, the §1-F table and the task rubric drift.

Authoring order: the 15 abilities are now locked (Part E) → author the 21 Acharya tasks' headline_abilities from that set → encode the same set as the engine's top15. One list, three consumers.

HContent issues found in the sample cards for Sutirtha — not engine blockers

Reading both cards line by line surfaced three things that are authoring/content, not engine work. Encoding proceeds regardless; these want Sutirtha's fix in the template/source:

  1. §6 improvement prose is Corporate-flavoured. Both educator cards' §6 says "candidate", "Corporate readiness", "workplace performance", "interview impression" — copied from the Corporate card, not re-authored for educators. (§4 tables and §5 correctly say "Acharya".)
  2. Headings still say "Academic Success". §4 reads "…That Drive Academic Success" and "higher academic achievement" — should be teaching/educator framing for these streams.
  3. Foundation and Senior share identical Part-B pillar benchmark + sample scores (Att 77.4/93, Apt 77.9/94, … Overall 77.5/93.5 — byte-identical), which looks copy-pasted. The ability-level benchmarks DO differ correctly (Foundation ≈90–94, Senior ≈91–97). Confirm whether the two streams intend the same pillar-level champion benchmark, or Senior's should be higher like its ability benchmarks.

§The graphs note — why "can't update the graphs" is a non-issue

Sutirtha noted she couldn't update the graphs. That is by design, not a blocker. The graphs are pure data-renders — the engine emits the numbers (pillar %, ability %, benchmark %, IntelliGraph zones) and the render draws the bars/charts from that data (the doc itself calls them "AI generated graph"). Nobody hand-edits a graph in a template. Once the Part-E data is encoded, the graphs redraw themselves correctly — including the single saffron benchmark series. Her template edits are the prose/headings (§H); the graphs follow the data automatically.
