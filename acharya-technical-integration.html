1 The discriminator

Add challenges.audience — enum student | acharya, default student, in $fillable (Challenge.php). Defined on the challenge because it's known at enrolment and every surface already resolves the Challenge, so the discriminator rides existing joins with near-zero new plumbing. Do not put a role on Student (a person can hold multiple enrolments — audience belongs to the product).

Two independent axes. audience = who it's for (student vs teacher). is_listed (new, default false for Acharya) = how it's sold (storefront vs institutional). Acharya = audience=acharya + is_listed=false. Keep them separate — don't overload one flag.
