How we measure

We don't promise specific marks or ranks. Anyone who does is being dishonest.

What we can tell you is exactly how the measuring is done — and how we keep one Yashodarshi's 82% meaning what another's does.

Step one

Scan

The Foundation 21-task system. A child does real things; a trained Yashodarshi observes and records.

Step two

Map

Observations are mapped across the 64 essential abilities — strengths named first, always.

Step three

Calibrate

Evaluators are calibrated against national benchmark standards, so a score means the same thing in Mysuru and in Mangaluru. Protocol to confirm — KMM.

Step four

Strengthen

A personalised year with a Yashodarshi, re-measured against her own earlier scores.

What we will not claim

  • We do not promise a mark, a rank or an admission. Outcomes depend on the child and on the year she puts in.
  • We do not rank your child against other people's children. The national benchmark calibrates our evaluators; it is not a ladder we put your daughter on.
  • Our figures come from our own framework and our own observations. Where a number appears, we will tell you how it was produced.
  • This is education and human development. It is not medical, diagnostic or healthcare, and "Success Doctor" is a brand expression, not a clinical claim.

Questions

Honest answers, for whoever is reading.

A sixteen-year-old and her mother are not asking the same thing. A twenty-six-year-old is asking neither.

Is this tuition or coaching?

No. YASHHO is not tuition, coaching, counselling or motivation. We develop the 64 inner abilities behind confidence, clarity and capability — working alongside your child's school, never replacing it.

Which age or class is it for?

From Class 5 onward — the stage where strengthening the root has the greatest long-term effect on academics, career clarity and emotional steadiness.

Do you guarantee better marks?

No, and anyone who does is being dishonest. We strengthen the abilities that improve performance, confidence and clarity. The ranges we show are observed, not promised, and depend on your child's engagement.

How will I know it is working?

Through her own scorecard, re-measured over the year. You will see where she was, where she is now, and what moved — compared with herself, not with another child.

Who exactly will work with my child?

Draft — needs the real answerA named Yashodarshi, whose photograph and background you will see before you begin, and who signs the scorecard she produces.

DraftIs this something being done to me?

No. Every task is yours to do, in your own life, and the scorecard is about you rather than about your class rank. Nobody is being compared with you.

DraftWill it tell me I am bad at things?

It leads with what you are genuinely strong at — most people are surprised by that part. Then it shows the one or two things standing in the way of those strengths.

DraftWill my parents see everything?

Yes — they are part of this, and the honest version works better than a managed one. What you tell your Yashodarshi in confidence stays with her.

DraftHow much time will it take from studying?

Very little, and it is built to fit around school. Real weekly hours to be confirmed.

DraftI am twenty-six and out of college. Is this for me?

Yes. The abilities that decide a career are the same ones that decided school, and they can still be measured and strengthened. Nothing here depends on being a child.

DraftI am preparing for KAS or IAS. What does this add?

Aspirants tell us the difference shows in comprehension, written structure and decision-making under pressure — the parts coaching assumes you already have. Adult pathway detail to be confirmed.

DraftI have a job and want to strengthen my core skills. Where do I start?

With the same scan, read against the corporate pathway rather than an exam. You get named strengths, the gaps holding them back, and a year with a Yashodarshi if you want one.

DraftWill I be ranked against schoolchildren?

No. You are measured against where you were. The benchmark exists to calibrate our evaluators, not to place you on a ladder.
