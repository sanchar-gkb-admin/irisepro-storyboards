Acharya — Business Requirements

Plain-language brief · the teacher-strengthening program · 2026-07-25

What Acharya is

Acharya is our program for teachers — the teaching community from pre-KG to professors. It runs the same 64-ability journey our students go through (do a set of tasks, get evaluated by a Yashodarshi, receive a scorecard), but the manual, the scoring, and the report are tuned for teachers instead of school students.

Why it matters: it's a B2B/institutional product — schools adopt it to strengthen their teachers. It's a new revenue line that rides on machinery we already have, so most of it is reuse, not rebuild.

What we need — and who provides it

Content from Sutirtha Sutirtha

  • The Acharya tasks + how they're scored — the set of tasks teachers complete, and which of the 64 abilities each task measures.
  • The teacher scorecard "lens" — what "average / strong / excellent" looks like for a teacher, teacher growth pathways (not student career roles), and teaching-impact wording.
  • Guidance for evaluators — how a Yashodarshi should judge a teacher's answer (what a strong response looks like).
  • The teacher manual — a short guide teachers read during onboarding, in English & Kannada.

From the team / admin Ops

  • Enrol teachers with their date of birth — so the system recognises them as adults (skips the parent-consent step meant for children).
  • The ₹4,000 + ₹800/month is handled by finance, offline — the system just records that the teacher is enrolled; there's no online payment for Acharya.

Build Engineering

  • Mark the product as "for teachers" and "not on the public store", wire the admin enrolment, and tune onboarding, evaluation, and the report to read correctly for a teacher.

How we'll know it's working (acceptance)

  • ✅ Admin can add a group of teachers and they receive a login.
  • ✅ A teacher logs in and onboards without being asked school-student questions, and reads a teacher guide.
  • ✅ Teachers complete the tasks and are evaluated normally.
  • ✅ Each teacher receives a scorecard that reads as a teacher's report — teacher benchmark, teacher pathways, no "Student"/class/exam framing.
  • ✅ Acharya never appears on the public website and can't be bought online.
  • ✅ The existing student experience is completely unchanged.

Timeline — what can start when

Teachers can be enrolled and start their tasks right away — that part needs nothing from Sutirtha. The one thing that must be ready before the first group finishes is the teacher scorecard content (tasks/rubric + the teacher lens), because that's what turns their completed work into a report.
